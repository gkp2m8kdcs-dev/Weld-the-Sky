Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Weld the Sky ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information needed to provide and improve the App:

  • Account Information: If you create an account, we may collect your email address and a display name.
  • User-Generated Content: Constellations, drawings, and preferences you create within the App.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device type, operating system, language, app version, and anonymized usage statistics.
  • Crash & Diagnostics: Crash reports and performance metrics to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App;
  • Save your constellations and progress across devices;
  • Respond to your support requests;
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues;
  • Improve features and user experience.

3. Local Storage

Weld the Sky primarily stores your constellations, settings, and progress locally on your device. You may choose to sync or back up this data to our servers by signing in or enabling cloud features.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services (such as analytics and crash reporting) that process limited data on our behalf. These services are bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

5. Children

Weld the Sky is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy in the App or via email when appropriate.
