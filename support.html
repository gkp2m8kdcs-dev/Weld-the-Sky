Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new constellation?

Tap an empty area of the sky to spawn a star, then drag from one star to another to weld a line between them. Any connected group of stars becomes your constellation.

Where is my data stored?

By default, your constellations and progress are saved on your device. If you sign in or enable cloud backup, your data will also sync to our secure servers so you can access it across devices.

I lost my constellations after reinstalling the app.

If you were not signed in to a cloud account, local data may have been cleared with the app. Reinstall and sign in to your account to enable automatic cloud backup going forward.

The app is running slowly or crashing.

Try the following:

  • Make sure you're running the latest version of Weld the Sky;
  • Restart the app and your device;
  • Free up storage space if your device is nearly full;
  • If the issue persists, contact us with your device model and OS version.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can request account and data deletion at any time by emailing our support address below. We will process your request within 30 days.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

We love hearing from you! Send bug reports, suggestions, or feedback to our support email. Please include your device model, OS version, and a clear description.

Still need help?

Reach out to our support team and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
